* Quesadilla Framework

** Base Components
- *Tortillas*: Flour provides better fold and hold; corn has more flavor but is more brittle
- *Cheese*: Melting varieties - Monterey Jack, cheddar, Oaxaca, or mix for best texture and flavor

** Protein Options (Optional)
- *Pre-cooked options*: Leftover chicken, steak, pork, ground beef, or beans
- *Quick-cook options*: Shrimp, thin-sliced steak, chorizo
- *Vegetarian*: Black or pinto beans, refried beans

** Vegetable Add-ins
- *Pre-cooked*: Bell peppers, onions, zucchini, corn
- *Raw*: Spinach, arugula, thinly sliced tomatoes
- *Kid-friendly tip*: Finely dice vegetables to "hide" them

** Prep-Ahead Components
- Batch cook proteins on weekends
- Pre-grate cheese and store in container with small amount of cornstarch to prevent clumping
- Sauté veggie mix and refrigerate for 3-4 days

** Assembly Methods
1. *Double-decker*: Tortilla → cheese → fillings → cheese → tortilla
2. *Fold-over*: Load ingredients on half, fold over (easier for kids to manage)

** Cooking Technique
1. *Griddle method*: Perfect for family meals; cook multiple at once
   - Medium heat (350°F), light butter or oil
   - Cook until golden (2-3 minutes per side)

2. *Cast iron method*: Best flavor development
   - Preheat skillet on medium-low 
   - Add assembled quesadilla, press gently
   - Flip when golden (about 3 minutes)

3. *Oven method*: For large batches
   - 375°F, place on sheet pans
   - Bake 8-10 minutes, flipping halfway

** Serving Options
- *Kid-friendly*: Cut into triangles with pizza cutter, serve plain or with mild salsa
- *Adult variations*: Include more complex add-ins and spicier salsas

** Budget Tips
- Buy cheese in bulk and grate yourself
- Use beans as protein extender
- Make your own seasoning blends instead of pre-packaged

** Make-Ahead Options
- Assemble multiple quesadillas and freeze between parchment paper
- Cook frozen directly on griddle (lower heat, longer time)
