#+TITLE: Low Country Boil

* Low Country Boil

** Equipment
- 8-quart stock pot
- Large slotted spoon
- Colander
- Newspaper or butcher paper for serving

** Core Ingredients
- 3 lbs crawfish, live or pre-cooked (frozen is OK in a pinch)
- 1.5 lbs small red potatoes, halved or quartered
- 3-4 ears corn, cut into 2-3 inch pieces
- 1 lb andouille sausage, cut into 2-inch chunks
- 1 large onion, quartered
- 2 lemons, halved

** Spice Mix
- 1/3 cup Cajun/Creole seasoning (Tony Chachere's, Zatarain's, or Slap Ya Mama)
- 2-3 bay leaves
- 1 tablespoon cayenne pepper (adjust for heat)
- 4-5 garlic cloves, smashed
- 2 tablespoons Old Bay seasoning (optional)
- 2 tablespoons whole peppercorns
- 1-2 tablespoons crystal hot sauce

** Purchasing Live Crawfish

Purchasing live crawfish can be intimidatting. Bring a cooler with you. Place the crawfish in their sack in the cooler, then place a towel on top. Put a bag of ice or frozen water bottles over the towel. You don't want them to drown, but you do want them to stay cold.

Drain meltwater frequently, and cook them the same day you buy them. Right before cooking rinse thoroughly. They should "wake up" from being so cold. Discard any that have died and do not cook them.

** Cooking Framework

1. *Start the Boil*: Fill pot 2/3 with water, add all spices, halved lemons (squeeze juice in first), and onion. Bring to a rolling boil.

2. *Layer in Ingredients*:
   - Add potatoes, boil 8-10 minutes until half-done
   - Add corn and sausage, boil 5 minutes
   - Add crawfish (if using live), boil 3-4 minutes until bright red
   - If using pre-cooked crawfish, add last and boil just 1-2 minutes to heat through

3. *The Critical Rest*: Turn off heat, cover, and let everything steep in the spicy broth for 15-20 minutes - this is where the magic happens!

4. *Drain and Serve*: Pour everything onto newspaper-covered table or large serving trays. Server with melted butter.

** Adaptations & Tips

- *Heat Level*: Adjust cayenne and hot sauce to your Cajun princess's preferred spice level
- *Seafood Swaps*: Add or substitute shrimp (2 lbs, 2-3 minutes cooking time) or crab legs
