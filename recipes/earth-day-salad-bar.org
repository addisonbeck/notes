* Earth Day Salad Bar Framework

*Theme:* Celebrating the earth's bounty! Focus on fresh, colourful, plant-forward options, minimize waste, and incorporate seasonal/local items where practical and budget-friendly.

*Goal:* Everyone builds their own satisfying meal-in-a-bowl.

*Core Components (Think in Categories):*

1.  *The Green Base (Choose 1-3):*
    *   *Kid-Friendly Crunch:* Romaine lettuce (chopped), Iceberg lettuce (shredded), Baby spinach (mild flavor).
    *   *Texture & Flavor:* Mixed spring greens, Arugula (for a peppery bite, maybe more for adults), Shredded cabbage (red/green for colour and crunch).
    *   /Prep:* Wash, spin dry, and chop/tear greens hours ahead. Store in bags with a paper towel.

2.  *Protein Power (Offer 2-3):* /Essential for a complete meal./
    *   *Kid Favorites:*
        *   Hard-boiled eggs (classic, easy, budget-friendly) - /Prep ahead./
        *   Shredded Chicken (Cook a whole chicken or breasts in the Instant Pot or Dutch Oven, shred) - /Prep ahead./
        *   Chickpeas (canned, rinsed - super budget-friendly, plant-based power) - /Minimal prep./
    *   *Other Options:*
        *   Cubed Tofu/Tempeh (marinated and quickly pan-fried on the cast iron/griddle) - /Prep marinade ahead, cook quickly./
        *   Lentils (cooked brown or green lentils) - /Prep ahead./
        *   Flaked Tuna/Salmon (canned, good quality) - /Minimal prep./
        *   Leftover cooked ham, turkey, or beef, cubed small.

3.  *Vibrant Veggies (Offer LOTS - 5+ raw/cooked):* /Think colour and variety!/
    *   *Raw & Ready (Kid magnets):* Cherry tomatoes (halved), Cucumber (sliced/diced), Carrots (shredded/matchsticks), Bell peppers (sweet varieties - red, yellow, orange, sliced thinly), Celery sticks.
    *   *Cooked & Cool:*
        *   Roasted Sweet Potatoes or Butternut Squash cubes (Dutch oven) - /Prep ahead./
        *   Steamed or Roasted Broccoli/Cauliflower florets (tender is key for kids) - /Prep ahead./
        *   Grilled Corn (kernels cut off the cob - use your griddle!) - /Prep ahead./
        *   Blanched Green Beans or Peas - /Prep ahead./
    *   *Flavor Accents:* Thinly sliced red onion, Radishes, Pickled beets (canned ok).
    *   /Prep:* Wash, chop, dice raw veggies ahead. Cook veggies ahead and let them cool.

4.  *Fruity Fun (Offer 1-2):* /Adds sweetness and appeals to kids./
    *   Berries (strawberries, blueberries - seasonal best!)
    *   Apple or Pear slices (toss with a tiny bit of lemon juice to prevent browning)
    *   Mandarin orange segments (canned is fine and easy)
    *   Grapes (halved for younger kids)
    *   Dried cranberries or raisins.
    *   Avocado cubes (healthy fats!)
    *   /Prep:* Wash/slice fruits just before serving if possible, or prep berries/canned fruit ahead.

5.  *Carb Comfort (Offer 1-2):* /Adds bulk and satisfaction./
    *   Croutons (Make your own from stale bread in the cast iron/oven - less waste!) - /Prep ahead./
    *   Cooked Quinoa or Farro - /Prep ahead./
    *   Small pasta shapes (rotini, shells) - /Prep ahead./
    *   Cornbread, crumbled - /Prep ahead./
    *   Pita bread wedges or tortilla strips on the side.

6.  *Crunch & Texture (Offer 2-3):* /Adds interest./
    *   Sunflower seeds (shelled)
    *   Pumpkin seeds (pepitas)
    *   Toasted nuts (sliced almonds, chopped walnuts - use cast iron for quick toasting) - /Toast ahead./
    *   Crispy onions (store-bought or homemade)
    *   Crushed tortilla chips.

7.  *Cheese (Offer 1-2):* /Flavor and fat./
    *   Shredded Cheddar or Monterey Jack (familiar for kids)
    *   Crumbled Feta or Goat Cheese (tangy)
    *   Cubed Mozzarella
    *   /Prep:* Shred/crumble ahead.

8.  *Dressings (Offer 2-3):* /Homemade is budget-friendly & healthier! Your Vitamix is perfect./
    *   *Creamy Classic:* Homemade Ranch or a simple Creamy Herb (yogurt/sour cream base, herbs, lemon juice, garlic).
    *   *Simple Vinaigrette:* Basic Balsamic, Red Wine, or Apple Cider Vinaigrette (Oil:Vinegar ratio ~ 3:1, pinch of salt, pepper, optional mustard/honey).
    *   *Kid-Friendly Sweet:* Honey Mustard Vinaigrette.
    *   *Earth Day Special:* Green Goddess Dressing (packed with fresh herbs!)
    *   /Prep:* Make dressings in the Vitamix up to 3 days ahead. Store in jars.

*Prep-Ahead Strategy:*

-   *2-3 Days Ahead:* Make dressings. Cook grains (quinoa, farro). Toast nuts/seeds. Make croutons.
-   *1 Day Ahead:* Cook and shred chicken. Hard boil eggs. Roast/steam veggies (sweet potatoes, broccoli, etc.). Wash and prep sturdy greens (romaine, cabbage). Cook pasta.
-   *Day Of:* Chop remaining raw veggies and fruits. Assemble the bar!

*Kid-Friendly Approach:*

-   *Familiarity:* Include several items you know they already like (shredded carrots, cucumber, cheese, ranch).
-   *Choice:* Let them build their /own/ plate. Even if it's just lettuce, chicken, cheese, and ranch – that's a win!
-   *Small Bowls:* Present toppings in smaller, accessible bowls.
-   *Deconstructed:* Offer some components (like chicken or bread) on the side, not /just/ for mixing in.

*Budget & Earth Day Notes:*

-   *Seasonal:* Focus on veggies and fruits that are in season – they're usually cheaper and taste better.
-   *Plant Power:* Lean into budget-friendly plant proteins like chickpeas and lentils.
-   *Whole Veggies:* Buy whole carrots, heads of lettuce, etc., and prep them yourself – less expensive than pre-cut.
-   *Waste Not:* Use veggie scraps (onion ends, carrot peels, celery butts) to make stock later. Turn stale bread into croutons. Use wilted greens in smoothies or sauté them.
-   *Homemade:* Dressings, croutons are much cheaper and often healthier made at home.
