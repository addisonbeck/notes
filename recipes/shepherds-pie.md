# Traditional Irish Shepherd's Pie Recipe

## Ingredients

### For the meat filling:
- 15g olive oil (1 tablespoon)
- 1 large onion, finely diced (about 150g)
- 2 carrots, peeled and diced (about 200g)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (about 6g)
- 680g lamb mince (traditional) or beef mince (alternative)
- 30g tomato paste (2 tablespoons)
- 15g Worcestershire sauce (1 tablespoon)
- 240ml beef or lamb stock (1 cup)
- 1g dried thyme (1 teaspoon)
- 1g dried rosemary (1 teaspoon)
- 150g frozen peas (1 cup)
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

### For the potato topping:
- 1.13kg russet potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks (2.5 pounds)
- 56g butter (4 tablespoons)
- 80ml warm milk (1/3 cup)
- 3g salt (1/2 teaspoon)
- 30g grated Irish cheddar cheese (optional) (1/4 cup)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).

2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onions and carrots, cook for 5 minutes until softened.

3. Add garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.

4. Add the lamb mince, breaking it up with a wooden spoon. Cook until browned, about 8-10 minutes.

5. Stir in the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, stock, thyme, and rosemary.

6. Reduce heat to low, cover and simmer for 15-20 minutes until sauce has thickened. Stir in frozen peas in the last 5 minutes.

7. Meanwhile, place potatoes in a large pot with salted water. Bring to a boil and cook until tender, about 15-20 minutes.

8. Drain potatoes well and return to pot. Add butter, warm milk, and salt. Mash until smooth and creamy.

9. Transfer meat mixture to a baking dish. Spread mashed potatoes evenly over the top. Use a fork to create ridges for a crispy surface.

10. If using, sprinkle grated cheese over the top.

11. Bake for 25-30 minutes until the top is golden brown and the filling is bubbling.

12. Let stand for 10 minutes before serving.

## Notes
- Traditional shepherd's pie uses lamb mince; cottage pie uses beef mince
- For extra flavor, add a splash of Irish stout to the meat mixture
- Serve with a side of soda bread for a complete Irish meal
