#+TITLE: Focaccia

* Focaccia
#+begin_quote
Original recipe from
https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/big-and-bubbly-focaccia-recipe
It has been modified a bit to work in a 10x14 Detroit Style Pizza pan
#+end_quote

** Ingredients
- Flour: 4 1/2 cups (540g)
- Water: 1 7/8 cups (450g)
- Olive oil: 3 tablespoons (45g) for the dough, plus extra for the pan
- Salt: 2 1/4 teaspoons
- Yeast: 3 teaspoons (or 1 tablespoon)
- Sugar: 1 1/2 teaspoons

** Bakers Percentage
- Flour: 100%
- Water: 83.3%
- Olive oil: 8.3%
- Salt: 2.5%
- Yeast: 1.7%
- Sugar: 1.1%

** Instructions
1. Mix all the ingredients together in a large bowl
2. Cover and autolyse for 15 minutes.
3. Perform a bowl fold
4. Wait 15 minutes
5. Perform a second bowl fold
6. Wait 15 minutes
7. Perform a 3rd bowl fold
8. Wait 15 minutes
9. Perform a 4th bowl fold
10. Cover the dough and let it rise for about an hour. It will be very
    puffy and may have a few bubbles on the surface.
11. Apply oil to your pan. Optionally add a strip of parchment that
    overhangs to help get the bread out later.
12. Gently transfer the dough to the pan. Handle it minimally.
13. Cover and proof for 1 to 1.5 hours. It should be marshmallowy and
    jiggly, and have spread to the edge of the pan.
14. Toward the end of the rise, preheat the oven to 475°F with racks in
    the upper and lower thirds.
15. Once the dough has risen dimple it.
16. Add toppings. Olive oil and flaky salt is a requirement but anything
    else works too.
17. Bake the focaccia on the lower rack for 30 to 35 minutes, until
    brown in the highest spots and golden in the crevices. If necessary,
    move the pan to the top rack and broil briefly for the final 1 to 2
    minutes, watching carefully, to achieve the desired color.
18. Remove the focaccia from the oven. Using the parchment tabs as
    handles, lift the focaccia out of the pan and transfer it to a wire
    rack or cutting board; remove the parchment strip. Turn off the oven
    and slide the focaccia back into the oven, directly on the lower
    rack, for 5 to 7 minutes, until the sides are golden brown and
    crisp. Remove the focaccia from the oven once again and transfer it
    to a wire rack to cool completely.
    

* Sourdough Focaccia

** Ingredients

*** Preferment
- 100g active sourdough starter
- 150g bread flour
- 150g water

*** Main Dough
- All of the preferment
- 350g bread flour
- 200-225g water (start with 200g, adjust as needed)
- 10g salt
- 30g olive oil (plus more for the pan and topping)

** Instructions

*Day 1 - Evening:*
1. Mix preferment ingredients until well combined
2. Cover and let sit at room temperature for 12 hours

*Day 2 - Morning:*
1. Mix preferment with remaining flour, 200g water, salt, and oil
2. Adjust hydration if needed - dough should be quite wet but manageable
3. Perform 3-4 sets of stretch and folds at 30-minute intervals
4. Let bulk ferment until puffy and increased in volume (2-3 hours depending on temperature)
5. Transfer to oiled container, cover, and place in refrigerator (8-24 hours)

*Day 2 - Evening or Day 3:*
1. Remove from refrigerator 2 hours before baking
2. Oil a sheet pan generously
3. Gently transfer dough to pan and stretch to fill (if it resists, let rest 20 minutes and try again)
4. Dimple with oiled fingertips, drizzle with more olive oil and add toppings
5. Final proof at room temperature until puffy (1-2 hours)
6. Bake at 450°F for 20-25 minutes until golden
