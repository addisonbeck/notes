#+TITLE: Deviled Eggs

* Ingredients
- Large eggs (preferably 1-2 weeks old for easier peeling)
- Mayonnaise
- Yellow or Dijon mustard
- Salt and pepper
- Optional add-ins:
  * Vinegar or pickle juice (for tang)
  * Paprika (for garnish)
  * Fresh herbs (dill, chives)
  * Hot sauce or cayenne (for heat)
* Equipment
- Pot for boiling eggs
- Ice bath setup
- Small bowl for filling
- Piping bag or spoon for filling
- Serving platter
* Instructions
1. Start eggs in cold water
2. Bring to a gentle boil
3. Remove from heat, cover, and let stand for 9-12 minutes (9 minutes for slightly creamier centers, 12 for fully set but still moist)
4. Transfer immediately to an ice bath to stop cooking
3. Gently cracking the shell all over and starting to peel from the air pocket (wider end)
5. Peeling under running water or in a bowl of water
6. Cut the hard-boiled egg in half lengthwise
7. Gently squeeze the half slightly from the sides
8. The yolk should pop out cleanly
0. Mash yolks thoroughly with a fork or press through a fine mesh sieve for ultimate creaminess
10. Mix wet ingredients gradually for perfect consistency - smooth but firm enough to pipe
11. Taste and adjust seasonings before filling egg whites
12. Fill the egg whites with the mixture using a piping bag or cut ziplock bag
13. Garnish
14. Refidgerate over night before serving

* Riffs

** St. Patricks Day

- To make the white green: before cutting soak them in a water and green food coloring mixture with a splash of vinegar. Use gel food coloring. Let them sit for a few hours in the fridge.
- To make the yolks green: incorprate green food coloring into the mixture
