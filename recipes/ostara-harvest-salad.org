* Ostara Harvest Salad 

** Ingredients

*** Base
- 6 cups mixed spring greens (baby spinach, arugula, tender lettuce)
- 1 cup fresh herbs (mint, parsley, chives - first spring herbs)

*** Protein (choose one or combine)
- 2 cups roasted lamb, sliced thinly
- 2 cups roasted chicken 
- 1½ cups cooked lentils or quinoa (plant-based option)
- 4 hard-boiled eggs, quartered (key Ostara symbol)

*** Spring Elements
- 1 cup early spring vegetables (radishes, spring onions, pea shoots)
- ½ cup edible spring flowers (violets, dandelion blossoms) if available
- ¼ cup fresh microgreens

*** Winter's End Components
- 1 cup roasted root vegetables (sweet potatoes, carrots, beets)
- ½ cup toasted nuts or seeds (representing planting season)
- ¼ cup dried fruits (last of winter stores)

*** Balance Elements (light/dark - equinox theme)
- ½ cup crumbled white cheese (goat cheese, feta)
- ¼ cup dark elements (olives, dark berries, or purple cabbage)

*** Spring Equinox Dressing
- 3 tablespoons good olive oil
- 1 tablespoon honey 
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 small garlic clove, minced
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: 1 teaspoon fresh herbs (chives, mint)

** Instructions

1. Prepare protein of choice and allow to cool slightly.
2. Roast root vegetables with olive oil, salt, and herbs until caramelized.
3. Arrange greens and herbs as the base in a large serving bowl.
4. Artfully arrange protein and all other ingredients in sections atop the greens.
5. Whisk dressing ingredients together and serve alongside.
6. Before serving, mention the balance of light and dark, and how the salad represents the transition from winter to spring.
