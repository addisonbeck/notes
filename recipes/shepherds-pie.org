* Traditional Irish Shepherd's Pie Recipe
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: traditional-irish-shepherds-pie-recipe
:END:
** Ingredients
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ingredients
:END:
*** For the meat filling:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: for-the-meat-filling
:END:
- 15g olive oil (1 tablespoon)
- 1 large onion, finely diced (about 150g)
- 2 carrots, peeled and diced (about 200g)
- Celery
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (about 6g)
- 680g lamb mince (traditional) or beef mince (alternative)
- 30g tomato paste (2 tablespoons)
- 15g Worcestershire sauce (1 tablespoon)
- 240ml beef or lamb stock (1 cup)
- 1g dried thyme (1 teaspoon)
- 1g dried rosemary (1 teaspoon)
- 150g frozen peas (1 cup)
- 100g chopped mushrooms
- Fish sauce
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Sherry
- Smoked paprika

*** For the potato topping:
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: for-the-potato-topping
:END:
- 1.13kg russet potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks (2.5 pounds)
- 56g butter (4 tablespoons)
- 80ml warm milk (1/3 cup)
- 3g salt (1/2 teaspoon)
- Parsley
- 30g grated Irish cheddar cheese (optional) (1/4 cup)
- 15g parmesan
- 60g cream cheese
- Whole grain mustard
- Paprika
- Green onions
- An egg

** Instructions
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: instructions
:END:
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onions, carrots, mushrooms, and celery.
   Cook until softened.
3. Add garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
4. Add the lamb mince, breaking it up with a wooden spoon. Cook until
   browned, about 8-10 minutes.
5. Deglaze with some wine
5. Stir in the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, stock, thyme,
   rosemary, fish sauce, and smoked paprika.
6. Reduce heat to low, cover and simmer for 15-20 minutes until sauce
   has thickened. Stir in frozen peas in the last 5 minutes.
7. Meanwhile, place potatoes in a large pot with salted water. Bring to
   a boil and cook until tender, about 15-20 minutes.
8. Drain potatoes well and return to pot. Add butter, warm milk, cream cheese and
   salt. Mash until smooth and creamy.
9. Transfer meat mixture to a baking dish. Spread mashed potatoes evenly
   over the top. Use a fork to create ridges for a crispy surface.
10. Brush with the egg
11. If using, sprinkle grated cheese over the top.
12. Bake for 25-30 minutes until the top is golden brown and the filling
    is bubbling.
13. Let stand for 10 minutes.
14. Top with green onions, parsely, and paprika
** Notes
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: notes
:END:
- Traditional shepherd's pie uses lamb mince; cottage pie uses beef
  mince
- For extra flavor, add a splash of Irish stout to the meat mixture
- Serve with a side of soda bread for a complete Irish meal
- Make ahead and refrigerate! Fully assemble and bake the next day.
