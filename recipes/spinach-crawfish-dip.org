* spinach artichoke crawfish dip

** ingredients:
- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1/4 cup grated romano cheese
- 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp dried basil (or 2 tsp fresh)
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- salt and pepper to taste
- 1 (14 oz) can artichoke hearts, drained and chopped
- 10 oz frozen chopped spinach, thawed and well drained
- 1 lb crawfish tails, peeled and cleaned
- 1/2 cup diced onion
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
- optional: dash of hot sauce or cayenne pepper
- optional: 2 tbsp chopped green onions for garnish

** instructions:
1. preheat oven to 350°f.

2. in a skillet, melt butter over medium heat. sauté onions until translucent, about 4-5 minutes.

3. add crawfish tails and cook for 2-3 minutes until just heated through. season with a pinch of salt and pepper. remove from heat.

4. in a large bowl, mix cream cheese, mayonnaise, sour cream, parmesan cheese, romano cheese, garlic, basil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until smooth and well combined.

5. make sure the spinach is thoroughly squeezed to remove excess moisture. fold the spinach, artichoke hearts, and crawfish/onion mixture into the cheese mixture.

6. stir in 1/2 cup of the mozzarella cheese (and hot sauce or cayenne if using).

7. transfer to an oven-safe dip dish or a 2-quart baking dish. top with the remaining 1/2 cup mozzarella cheese.

8. bake for 25-30 minutes until bubbly and cheese is melted and lightly browned.

9. garnish with chopped green onions if desired.

10. serve hot with warm pita bread, naan, baguette slices, or tortilla chips.

this rich, creamy dip maintains its scoopable consistency while incorporating the crawfish for added protein, making it substantial enough to serve as a light meal when paired with plenty of bread for dipping.
