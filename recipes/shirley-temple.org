* Shirley Temples

I'm making these for mother's day 2025

* Non-Alcoholic: 

This is the one the kids (and often adults!) love.

1. *Fill the Glass:* Fill a tall glass (like a Collins or highball) with ice.
2. *Add the Fizz:* Pour in about 6-8 oz of chilled Ginger Ale /or/ Lemon-Lime Soda (like Sprite/7-Up). Ginger Ale is more traditional, but lemon-lime is very popular and kid-friendly. Use whatever you prefer or have on hand.
3. *Add the Grenadine:* Slowly drizzle in about 0.5 - 1 oz of grenadine. It will sink initially, creating a nice visual effect. Adjust the amount based on how sweet you like it – start with less, you can always add more.
4. *Gentle Stir (Optional):* Give it a very gentle stir just to slightly combine, or leave it layered for visual appeal.
5. *Garnish:* Add one or two maraschino cherries. A slice or wedge of orange is also a classic touch.

* Alcoholic Version:

This is the simple adaptation for adults.

1.  *Fill the Glass:* Fill a tall glass with ice.
2.  *Add the Spirit:* Pour in 1.5 oz of Vodka. This is the most common choice. Light rum could also work if you prefer.
3.  *Add the Fizz:* Top with about 4-6 oz of chilled Ginger Ale or Lemon-Lime Soda.
4.  *Add the Grenadine:* Drizzle in about 0.5 oz of grenadine.
5.  *Gentle Stir:* Stir gently to combine the ingredients.
6.  *Garnish:* Garnish with maraschino cherries and/or an orange slice. A lime wedge is also a nice addition here for extra tartness.
