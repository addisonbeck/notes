# Focaccia

> Original recipe from https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/big-and-bubbly-focaccia-recipe
> It has been modified a bit to work in a 10x14 Detroit Style Pizza pan

## Ingredients

* Flour: 4 1/2 cups (540g)
* Water: 1 7/8 cups (450g)
* Olive oil: 3 tablespoons (45g) for the dough, plus extra for the pan
* Salt: 2 1/4 teaspoons
* Yeast: 3 teaspoons (or 1 tablespoon)
* Sugar: 1 1/2 teaspoons

## Bakers Percentage

Flour: 100%
Water: 83.3%
Olive oil: 8.3%
Salt: 2.5%
Yeast: 1.7%
Sugar: 1.1%

## Instructions

1. Mix all the ingredients together in a large bowl
1. Cover and autolyse for 15 minutes.
1. Perform a bowl fold
1. Wait 15 minutes
1. Perform a second bowl fold
1. Wait 15 minutes
1. Perform a 3rd bowl fold
1. Wait 15 minutes
1. Perform a 4th bowl fold
1. Cover the dough and let it rise for about an hour. It will be very puffy
   and may have a few bubbles on the surface.
1. Apply oil to your pan. Optionally add a strip of parchment that overhangs
   to help get the bread out later.
1. Gently transfer the dough to the pan. Handle it minimally.
1. Cover and proof for 1 to 1.5 hours. It should be marshmallowy and jiggly,
   and have spread to the edge of the pan.
1. Toward the end of the rise, preheat the oven to 475°F with racks in the
   upper and lower thirds.
1. Once the dough has risen dimple it.
1. Add toppings. Olive oil and flaky salt is a requirement but anything else
   works too.
1. Bake the focaccia on the lower rack for 30 to 35 minutes, until brown in
   the highest spots and golden in the crevices. If necessary, move the pan
   to the top rack and broil briefly for the final 1 to 2 minutes, watching
   carefully, to achieve the desired color.
1. Remove the focaccia from the oven. Using the parchment tabs as handles,
   lift the focaccia out of the pan and transfer it to a wire rack or cutting
   board; remove the parchment strip. Turn off the oven and slide the
   focaccia back into the oven, directly on the lower rack, for 5 to 7
   minutes, until the sides are golden brown and crisp. Remove the focaccia
   from the oven once again and transfer it to a wire rack to cool
   completely.
