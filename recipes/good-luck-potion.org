#+TITLE: Finnegan's Good Luck Potion

> One sip brings good fortune, but be careful - too much luck can be tricky!

* Ingredients
- 1 cup clear corn syrup
- 1/4 cup water
- 10-15 drops green food coloring (or mix blue and yellow for a more magical green)
- 1/2 teaspoon peppermint extract
- 1/4 teaspoon almond extract
- 1/8 teaspoon cardamom powder
- Pinch of edible pearl dust or luster dust (optional, for shimmer)
- A few drops of glycerin (helps prevent crystallization)
- Small decorative bottles for serving

* Instructions
1. In a saucepan, combine the corn syrup and water. Heat on low until fully combined.
2. Remove from heat and let cool for a few minutes.
3. Add green food coloring until you reach your desired shade of lucky green.
4. Mix in the peppermint extract, almond extract, and cardamom powder.
5. If using, add a pinch of edible pearl dust and a few drops of glycerin.
6. Stir thoroughly until all ingredients are fully incorporated.
7. Pour into decorative bottles while still slightly warm.
8. Allow to cool completely before sealing.

* Why this works
- Corn syrup is a preservative and won't spoil when stored properly
- The combination of peppermint, almond and cardamom creates an unusual but pleasant flavor
- The thick consistency makes it look magical and potion-like
- The pearl dust (if used) adds a magical shimmer
- The glycerin helps maintain consistency over time
