* Baked Macaroni and Cheese
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: baked-macaroni-and-cheese
:END:
** Ingredients
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ingredients
:END:
- 1 1/2 pounds grated sharp cheddar cheese (680g; 6 cups)
- 8 ounces grated Gruyère cheese (225g; 2 cups)
- 1 pound (450g) elbow macaroni
- 1 cup panko bread crumbs (2 1/2 ounces; 72g)
- 5 tablespoons (75g) unsalted butter, divided
- 4 teaspoons sodium citrate (1 ounce; 20g), see notes
- 1 teaspoon (5ml) hot sauce, such as Frank's RedHot
- 1/2 teaspoon mustard powder
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- Kosher salt
- Some shredded chicken (optional)
- 630g water

** Directions
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: directions
:END:
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (204°C). Omit this step if making ahead of
   time.
2. To cook the pasta: in a medium pot of salted boiling water, cook
   elbow macaroni until just shy of al dente, about 2 minutes less than
   cooking time indicated on package.
3. Drain, then transfer pasta to a large mixing bowl and toss with 2
   tablespoons (30g) butter until butter is melted and pasta is evenly
   coated. Set aside.
4. To make the sauce: in a large saucepan, bring 630g water to a simmer.
   Whisk in sodium citrate until fully dissolved. While maintaining a
   gentle simmer, add cheddar cheese in small increments, whisking
   constantly to incorporate each addition before adding the next. When
   all the cheese is added and the sauce is smooth and glossy, whisk in
   hot sauce, mustard powder, and garlic powder. Season with salt, if
   necessary.
5. Scrape cheese sauce into pasta and mix until evenly coated. Let cool
   slightly, then add grated Gruyère and mix well. Scrape pasta into a
   greased 9- by 13-inch baking dish and smooth surface into an even
   layer over the shredded chicken.
6. Casserole can be assembled through this step, then covered and stored
   in the refrigerator for up to five days before baking. Increase
   baking time by 15 minutes if refrigerated.
7. Add panko to a small mixing bowl. Melt remaining 3 tablespoons
   butter, then add to panko and mix until evenly coated. Season with
   salt. Scatter panko all over surface of mac and cheese in an even
   layer. Bake on top rack of oven until browned and bubbling, about 45
   minutes (ovens can vary; check often to prevent top from burning).
8. Let mac and cheese rest 15 minutes, then serve. Leftover mac and
   cheese can be refrigerated for up to 5 days; it reheats surprisingly
   well in the microwave or oven.

** Riffs
*** St. Patrick's Day Macaroni And Cheese
**** Additions to your ingredient list:
- 1 cup fresh basil leaves, packed
- 2-3 cloves garlic
- 2 tablespoons pine nuts (optional)
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- Green food coloring (4-8 drops, depending on desired intensity)
**** Modifications to the directions:
1. *Make a quick basil mixture:* While your pasta is cooking, combine fresh basil, garlic, pine nuts (if using), and olive oil in a food processor. Pulse until finely chopped but not completely smooth.
2. Make the sauce a bit thinner
3. *Modify step 4:* After you've made your cheese sauce (after adding the hot sauce, mustard powder, and garlic powder), fold in your basil mixture. Stir until well combined.
4. *Add food coloring:* Once the basil is incorporated, add green food coloring drops one at a time, stirring between each addition, until you reach your desired shade of green. Start with 4 drops and add more if needed. The cheese sauce will appear slightly darker when wet than it will in the final baked dish.
5. *Continue as normal:* Proceed with the rest of the recipe as written, coating your pasta with the green cheese sauce and finishing with the panko topping.
